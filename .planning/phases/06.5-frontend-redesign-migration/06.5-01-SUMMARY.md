## Decisions Made

- Used native `select` and `textarea` wrappers instead of adding dependencies.
- Kept compatibility aliases for existing Button variants so current pages do not break during migration.
- Kept `.dark` mapped to the same warm tokens for now because the redesign replaces the dark baseline rather than preserving a dark theme.
